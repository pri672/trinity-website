A place to think clearly

Trinity College was founded on a simple idea: education works best when people know each other. Our classes stay small on purpose, so students argue, question, and build things alongside faculty who know their names.

Graduates leave with more than a transcript — they leave with a habit of asking better questions.

Our Programs

Undergraduate and graduate degrees across four faculties.

Science & Technology

Computer science, physics, biotechnology and applied mathematics, with lab access from the first semester.

Business & Management

Finance, marketing and entrepreneurship, taught through case work and a required industry placement.

Arts & Humanities

Literature, history, philosophy and media studies for students who want to read closely and write well.

Health Sciences

Nursing, public health and nutrition, with clinical rotations at partner hospitals across the region.

Life at Trinity

There is a lot happening between lectures.

40+ Student Clubs

Debate, robotics, theatre, hiking and everything in between — or start your own with a dozen signatures.

On-Campus Housing

Residence halls a short walk from every lecture theatre, with study lounges open around the clock.

Sports & Wellness

A full gym, courts, and intramural leagues that welcome beginners as readily as varsity athletes.
